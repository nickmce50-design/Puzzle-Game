# Puzzle-Game
My 2d Puzzle game/ Platformer game
Made by Nicholas McEntee

Targeted Experience
I wanted to make a 2D Platformer with a medieval fantasy style. I wanted to make a semi frustrating platformer that tries to mess with you a bit but not to frustrating. I also didn't want to make it to easy, so you need to find the key and enter the correct area to either advance levels or win the game. 



Puzzle Design Jounral
The Puzzle I spent the most time on, enjoyed the most, and was frustrated in making, was the first level. I had a hard time trying to figure out what I wanted to do. At first, I started out wanting to make it a 2-player puzzle game like Fire Boy and Water Girl from cool math games, then I decided against that, and I went with a single player 2d platformer. I always enjoyed watching/playing rage bait games, which whole goal was to try to trick your mind into thinking what the next hazard was going to be. Through some playtesting, I found out that some of the traps that I set up were too unexpected or just didn't fit the overall enjoyability, so I took some tricks and traps out of the first level. But I found out that people liked that the second level doesn't have any tricks on it, because they all thought it did which changed their playstyle. One of the main problems I had with the first level was figuring out both the keys and the enemy movements, but with help from online and the professors; I was able to get it done.
